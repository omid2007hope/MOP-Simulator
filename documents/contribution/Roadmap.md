## Next Milestones
1. **2D Trajectory & Path Deviation**:
   - Calculate pitch/yaw bending moments as a function of depth.
   - Simulate trajectory curving when attacking non-homogeneous multi-layer targets at oblique angles.
2. **Multi-Layer Target Strata**:
   - Support layered target profiles in `targets.json` (e.g., Soil -> Reinforced Concrete -> Granite).
3. **Enhanced Hydrodynamic Modeling**:
   - Advanced cavity expansion equations for hypervelocity impacts (> 2000 m/s).
