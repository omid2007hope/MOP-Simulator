## Failure Thresholds
1. **Pressure Yield (Crush)**:
   Triggered when dynamic pressure exceeds casing yield strength ($P_{dyn} > \sigma_y$).
2. **Hydrodynamic Limit**:
   For extreme high-velocity impacts ($v > 1200\ m/s$), penetration depth approaches the Alekseevskii-Tate hydrodynamic density ratio limit:

   $$ L_{max} = L \sqrt{\frac{\rho_p}{\rho_t}} $$

3. **Structural Bending (Snap / J-Hooking)**:
   Occurs during oblique impacts when differential normal stress generates a bending moment exceeding the section modulus of the casing.
