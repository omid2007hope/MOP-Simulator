## Thermal Model
During high-velocity penetration, sliding friction transfers thermal energy across the outer casing into the explosive filler.

If outer casing temperature or shock compression energy exceeds the explosive filler auto-ignition threshold ($T > T_{critical}$), early detonation occurs before maximum penetration depth is reached.
