## Governing Equations
Penetration into semi-infinite concrete targets is governed by cavity expansion theory:

$$ F_z = -\frac{\pi D^2}{4} \left( S f_c' + N \rho v^2 \right) $$

Where:
- $D$: Projectile diameter ($m$)
- $f_c'$: Unconfined compressive strength of target ($Pa$)
- $S$: Empirical target strength coefficient
- $N$: Nose shape factor (function of Caliber Radius Head - CRH)
- $\rho$: Target density ($kg/m^3$)
- $v$: Instantaneous velocity ($m/s$)
