## Governing Oblique Motion
When a penetrator strikes a target at an angle of obliquity $\theta$ and angle of attack $\alpha$, asymmetric normal forces generate pitch acceleration:

$$ I_y \ddot{\theta} = M_{bending}(z) $$

This causes path curving (J-hooking) as depth increases, reducing axial penetration depth while increasing shear strain on the nose.
